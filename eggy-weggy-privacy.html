About the versions covered. The short version

  • You can play offline and create a local nickname and avatar without a Google account. Puzzles, progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • If you separately enable a configured account service and sign in, Google Firebase processes account information, your chosen profile, private backups and friend/score records. After a successful cloud save or confirmed restore, automatic checkpoints run while the account is connected; an existing remote save or conflict is reviewed before replacement.
  • Friends are optional. Pending request participants can see your game profile; only approved friends can compare your level scores. There is no public player directory or chat.
  • Children, teens and users of unknown age cannot enable cloud or friends in this version. A supported parent-managed account and validated consent process are not configured; an age answer or local maths question is not parental authorisation.
  • You can play the puzzles without watching adverts. If you choose an optional advert, Google may process information to provide it.
  • We ask for an age range for advertising choices, rather than your birthday. Children, teens and players whose age is unknown receive child-directed, non-personalised advertising requests. All requests have a G content limit.
  • Problem reports and backup files are shared only when you take an action to share them.

For younger players: ask a parent or trusted adult before sending us a report or personal information. You can skip adverts and keep playing.

2. Information on your device

Eggy Weggy stores information locally so that you can leave and return to the game. This includes:

  • Your current puzzle, scores, stars, unlocked levels, challenges and rewards.
  • Coins, power-ups, lives and refill times, along with records that prevent the same reward or transaction being applied twice.
  • Sound, music, vibration, display, motion and control preferences.
  • Your selected age range, advertising privacy settings and separate cloud opt-in choice.
  • A locally chosen username and structured egg avatar, plus account-link and cloud revision information if you use the optional account service. Google authentication credentials are handled by the native authentication SDKs, separately from the puzzle screen and portable backups.
  • A rolling record of up to 300 recent attempts, including level, outcome, moves, score, stars, power-up use and when goals were completed in terms of moves. This helps explain difficulty or problems.

Without cloud opt-in, your puzzle save stays on the device unless you choose to export or share it. Configured account features send the information described below to Google's Firebase services. There is no online chat, contact-book import or publicly searchable leaderboard. The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location. Network services may receive your IP address and infer a general location from it.

3. Optional account, cloud saves and friends

Your choice and availability

You can create a nickname and egg avatar in Profile and keep playing offline without signing in. Cloud save is available from Profile or Settings; Friends is available from Profile. The online service requires a configured Firebase project, internet access, an explicit native opt-in and Google sign-in. Merely opening Profile, reading this notice or calling the passive account-status check does not initialise Firebase.

When the service is configured and an age range has not been chosen, an account-setup action first offers the neutral age choices without requesting advertising consent, loading an advert or initialising Firebase. A separate cloud opt-in follows the eligibility check.

This version allows the configured online flow only for a saved, known-adult age profile. Children, teens and unknown-age users remain blocked because a validated parent-managed service is not configured. An adult selection is not identity or age verification and is not parental consent. Opting into cloud does not opt you into advertising or purchases.

Google identity and your game profile

Google sign-in and Firebase Authentication process the Google account identity and credentials needed to authenticate you. Their native SDKs can receive account identifiers, email and provider profile information and handle sign-in tokens. The puzzle's JavaScript receives an opaque account-link identifier and your chosen game profile, not Google email, provider display name, profile photo or authentication tokens. We do not put those provider details in game profiles, portable backups or app error messages.

The account profile stored in Firestore contains your chosen username, structured egg avatar, randomly generated friend code and update time. Choose a nickname, not your real name, email address, phone number or other personal information. An avatar uses the game's preset parts; this feature does not upload personal photos or accept image URLs.

Private cloud backups

A cloud save uploads the complete checked portable backup: your board, campaign records, preferences, earned coin balance, owned power-ups and relevant one-time game-reward history. It is associated with your Firebase account, a revision and a server update time. Lives, advertising allowances, trusted purchase/reward receipts and Google credentials do not transfer with it. The recent-attempt diagnostic journal is not a cloud analytics feed.

When you choose Save to cloud, the game checks for an existing remote save before it can replace it. It does not silently overwrite that save. You choose whether to load it or replace it with this device's progress. After a successful save or confirmed restore, the game also schedules automatic cloud checkpoints after durable local progress or profile changes and when the app returns to the foreground. Signing in alone does not enable these automatic checkpoints. A conflicting remote revision pauses automatic replacement until you review it. A failed or offline request is not shown as a successful cloud save.

Load from cloud requires confirmation and validates the backup before replacing your board and permitted wallet data. Balances are replaced rather than added together, and local lives and trusted receipts remain on the device. Cloud recovery is not proof of a paid purchase or a transfer of an advertising entitlement. Export a local backup before replacing progress if you want to keep a separate copy.

Player access rules keep cloud backups private to their owning account; friends cannot read your board or wallet. The studio and its service providers may access service records where needed to operate and secure the service, answer a valid request or meet a legal duty. Cloud saving is not end-to-end encryption.

Friends and score comparisons

Friends starts off for a linked identity on this device. Enable friends is a separate choice that explains that approved friends can see your game username, avatar and level scores, then allows publication of those game records. Cloud opt-in alone is not this friends choice. If the account already has friendships or published records from another session, those server records remain until removed.

You choose whether to share your random friend code. A signed-in player needs the exact code to send a request; there is no name search or public profile directory. Profiles can be read by the owner, approved friends and participants in a pending request so that a request can be identified. A profile includes the chosen username, avatar and friend code.

A request does not grant score access. After the recipient accepts, approved friends can compare the stored best score and stars for individual campaign levels, alongside game usernames and avatars. These are game records submitted by players' devices, not a public or independently verified competition. The service also stores sender/recipient account identifiers, request status, accepted friend links and timestamps.

You can decline a pending request or remove a friend. Removing the friendship ends future reciprocal access to scores through the service. It cannot recall information someone already copied or photographed. Do not share friend codes or personal information with people you do not want to connect with.

Sign-out, opt-out and deletion

Signing out or turning off cloud signs out the native account session and stops new cloud operations. It does not delete previously saved account data or friendships and does not revoke approved friends' access to previously shared profiles and scores. Remove a friend or delete the game account to end that access. Firestore uses an in-memory cache for this feature; authentication SDKs manage their own native session information while the account is connected.

Use Delete cloud account in the account controls to request removal of the game account. A fresh Google authentication is required. The app first removes the account's cloud save, scores, profile, friend-code record and discoverable participant requests/friend links, then deletes its Firebase Authentication account. A malformed or orphaned relationship reference that is no longer linked from the account may require separate studio backend cleanup; a one-sided reference does not grant profile or score access under the reciprocal-friend rules. If authentication, connectivity or cleanup fails, the app must report that the deletion did not complete; it does not report success merely because the phone signed out. You can also contact the privacy email for help. Never send passwords or sign-in tokens.

Deleting this game account does not delete your Google account or your progress stored on the phone. Clear local game data separately if you want to remove it. Copies you exported or other people saved must be handled separately.

4. Optional adverts

Eggy Weggy uses Google's Mobile Ads service, also known as AdMob, for optional rewarded adverts, and Google's User Messaging Platform for applicable privacy choices. The current app uses Mobile Ads SDK 25.4.0 and User Messaging Platform 4.0.0. An advert may offer a life, extra moves or a virtual present. You can check a present's available prizes and odds before choosing the advert. You choose whether to request one; normal puzzle play does not require an advert. Only the SDK's earned-reward callback can create an advert reward. Declining an advert, a failed load or an unearned dismissal does not create a reward or use an unclaimed present opportunity. The testing preview uses Google's official sample adverts; live commercial advertising is disabled.

When an advertising or consent service is used, Google may process your IP address, IP-derived approximate location, device and app information, advert interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostic information about performance. Identifiers available to Google's services depend on the device, age treatment, privacy choices and service configuration. The current app removes Android advertising-ID and advertising-services permissions for every age group, and disables the SDK's publisher first-party ID before loading an advert. Publisher first-party ID is separate from App Set ID; these controls do not mean that an SDK processes no data or no other permitted identifier.

Google uses advertising information to deliver and measure adverts, maintain its services and help prevent fraud. Adult advertising may be personalised only where permitted and where any required choices allow it. Child, teen and unknown-age requests use child-directed, non-personalised treatment and disable publisher personalisation. Every age group has a G content limit. Non-personalised adverts still involve some processing of information; they are not a promise that no data is collected.

The app does not request or preload adverts at startup. An explicit advertising action starts the age and privacy checks. Where applicable, the adult privacy flow refreshes Google's consent information before each requested advert and presents any required consent form. If the required privacy check fails, that advert is unavailable. The current official-sample testing flow may not show publisher-configured consent messages; it still uses the selected age restrictions. Adult players can review applicable choices using Settings → Privacy → Advertising choices → Ad privacy choices. The native Privacy screen also offers Choose age range. You can decline optional adverts and continue playing.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Children and age choices

Eggy Weggy can be played by children and adults. You can start a puzzle without giving an age range. Before an optional advertising flow, when opening the privacy controls or when starting account setup, the app offers Under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, and Prefer not to say, with no answer preselected. It saves the range on the device and does not ask for an exact date of birth.

For under-18 and unknown-age profiles, the published app applies Google's TFAT CHILD age-restricted treatment before SDK initialisation and advert loads. Requests are non-personalised, publisher advertising personalisation is disabled, and a G content limit applies. Adult consent forms are not presented to these profiles. An unanswered, unavailable or unreadable age profile is treated conservatively, not as an adult. These controls do not establish parental consent or verify that a person is a parent.

New purchases remain unavailable to under-18 and unknown-age profiles. We do not use local puzzle records to build marketing profiles of children. A parent or guardian can contact us about information a child has sent, including a request to remove it. We may ask for the minimum information needed to identify the request and establish the person's authority.

6. Purchases

Real-money checkout is disabled in the published testing preview. Coins are virtual game currency, and spending earned coins on lives, extra moves or power-ups is separate from a real-money purchase. Coins and power-ups can be earned through play. The app contains foundations for Google Play purchases, but this does not mean paid products are available.

If paid products are enabled in a future approved build, checkout will use Google Play's displayed prices and its applicable purchase authentication and family-approval controls. Selecting an adult age in the game is not parental authorisation. The app's purchase foundations use product IDs, tokens and transaction state to verify delivery and prevent duplicate grants; the game does not ask for payment-card details. Before enabling checkout, we will update this policy for the actual purchase, verification, recovery and retention arrangements.

7. Support and backup files

Problem reports

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message and attachments. The game's Report a problem feature prepares your description, app version, current level and puzzle status, plus recent totals for that level. It excludes the full board, wallet, purchase details and game-account information.

A report is not sent automatically. Copy report puts it on your device clipboard and remains available to every age group. Open report on GitHub is available only to a saved, known-adult profile; children, teens and unknown or unreadable age profiles cannot use that action. The app checks the current age and privacy revision again before opening the browser. This is an age restriction, not parental verification.

The GitHub link contains the prepared report text, so opening it sends that text to the browser and GitHub before you decide whether to submit an issue. GitHub may require an account and access to the private testing repository, and handles that account and access information under its own policy. Review the report and its audience before opening it; use the privacy email for private questions. Do not include passwords, payment details or personal information about children. Copying a report alone does not open or send a GitHub report; any other app you subsequently choose to share it with has its own practices.

Backups you choose to create

Export backup creates a file using the location you select. It can include your current board, progress, preferences, coins, owned power-ups and relevant one-time game-reward history. Lives, advertising allowances and trusted purchase/reward receipts stay on the device. Google sign-in credentials are not included. Exporting a file does not itself send that file to Boontang Games; separately enabled cloud checkpoints use the account service described above.

If you choose a cloud-storage location or share a backup, that service processes the file under its own terms. Exported copies remain in the chosen location until you delete them. Android's file picker grants access to the particular file you select; the app does not need access to all your files.

8. Why information is used

Where UK data protection law applies, the relevant purposes and lawful bases are:

  • Running the game and remembering choices: our legitimate interests in providing the game you request, saving your progress and applying appropriate privacy settings. We limit this processing to what is needed and balance those interests against your rights and, particularly, children's interests.
  • Optional advertising and privacy choices: consent where required for advertising storage, access or personalisation. Where consent is required, it must be obtained before that processing. Age restrictions and applicable regional rules also apply.
  • Optional account services: our legitimate interests in providing and protecting the saving and friend features you request. The separate cloud opt-in controls whether that service is enabled; it is not consent for advertising or proof of parental authority.
  • Support and security: our legitimate interests in answering questions, investigating faults, protecting the service and preventing duplicate rewards or abuse, balanced against your rights and, particularly, children's interests.
  • Legal duties: processing necessary to meet an applicable legal obligation, for example responding to a valid legal request.

We do not sell your puzzle saves or local play notes. You do not have to create an online account, add friends, send a support report, export a backup or watch an advert to play. Without local storage, however, the app cannot reliably keep your progress between sessions.

10. Keeping and deleting information

On your device: saves, settings and local transaction records remain until replaced through normal use or removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Recent play notes roll over after 300 attempts. Android automatic app backup is disabled in the reviewed build. Clearing data may permanently remove local progress, coins, lives and receipts. Recovery is possible only from a valid backup you retained or a successful cloud save linked to an accessible account; signing in does not recover progress that was never uploaded.

Optional account records: the current cloud save is replaced when you successfully save a newer revision. Account profiles, scores, friend codes and relationships remain until you remove them or complete account deletion; no automatic expiry has been configured. Signing out, opting out, clearing app storage or uninstalling does not itself delete server records. The deletion flow removes the listed game records before the Firebase Authentication account. There is no automatic server-side cascade. The client cannot discover every malformed or orphaned peer-only relationship reference, and those references may need operator cleanup. Deleting an authentication account through another route does not itself remove its database records. Contact us if cleanup is interrupted or an account is inaccessible. Google's operational logs and any provider-managed backup retention follow its applicable service terms and project settings; deletion of every provider-held copy is not promised to be instantaneous.

Exported or shared files: delete these separately from wherever you saved or shared them. Clearing the app does not remove a backup in another location or a report already sent.

Support information held by us: we keep it for as long as reasonably needed to answer the request, resolve related faults or complaints, and meet any applicable legal duties. We then delete it or remove identifying details. The nature of the query, whether an issue remains open, and any legal retention requirement determine the period.

Information held by providers: Google, GitHub and other providers apply their own retention rules. Deleting local app data does not itself delete information already held by them.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on the law and circumstances, you may ask to access, correct or erase personal information held about you, restrict its use, or receive a portable copy. Contact the privacy address above. Parents and guardians can also contact us about their child's information.

Your right to object: you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will consider the request in accordance with the applicable law. You can also withdraw consent where processing relies on it, without affecting earlier lawful processing. Use the advertising privacy controls for applicable ad choices. You can separately turn off optional cloud or request game-account deletion through the account controls, or contact us about information held by Boontang Games.

We cannot remotely read or erase a save held only on your device. You can remove it through Android's app-storage controls, and separately delete exported backups. We may need limited information to locate a support record or verify a request; do not send your entire save unless it is needed.

You can complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office, or another data protection authority available to you. You do not have to contact us before raising a complaint.

12. Security

The app uses Android's application storage and limits the bundled puzzle screen's network and file access. Native account, advertising and privacy services operate separately; blocking network access in the puzzle WebView does not stop those SDK connections. Firebase cloud access is authenticated and limited by database rules; native Firestore caching is in memory. No Firebase Analytics dependency is included for account features. Cleartext app networking and Android automatic app backup are disabled. Google describes its advertising SDK connections as encrypted in transit; this does not mean exported backup files are encrypted or that every destination you choose has the same protection. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Protect your device and any backup files you export.

13. Changes and website privacy

We will update this policy when the game's information practices change, including before enabling materially different advertising, purchases, account access or cloud processing. The published policy will state its effective or updated date. Where the law requires a new notice or consent, we will provide it before the relevant processing.

Publishing this policy does not launch public accounts, enable live adverts or purchases, submit a Google Play Data safety declaration or establish Families compliance. The account service remains a private adult testing feature. Device and service verification, the operational deletion process and public account-deletion request resources must be completed before a public account launch.

For the studio website itself, read the separate website privacy notice.
