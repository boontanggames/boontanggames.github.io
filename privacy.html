Website privacy.

Information about our studio website and how it operates.

Website information. For the Android game, read the separate Eggy Weggy privacy policy.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who is responsible

Boontang Games is the trading name of Kenneth Burton, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. Kenneth Burton is the data controller for personal information handled by Boontang Games. Contact boontanggames@gmail.com for support or privacy questions.

About this website

This is a static informational website about the studio and Eggy Weggy. It has no advertising scripts, analytics, accounts, subscription form or contact form. It does not use tracking cookies or external fonts.

Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted with GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub receives technical access information, such as your IP address and browser request, to serve the site and protect its infrastructure. GitHub handles this information under GitHub's General Privacy Statement. Boontang Games does not operate a separate website analytics service.

If you email us

We receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use Google's Gmail service to manage enquiries. We use this information for our legitimate interests in answering questions, providing support and resolving problems, balanced against your privacy rights, or to meet an applicable legal obligation.

We keep correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the enquiry, manage related faults or complaints, and meet any applicable legal duties. Whether a matter remains open, the nature of the enquiry and any legal retention requirement determine the period. We then delete it or remove identifying details. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

Other services and international processing

Links may lead to external services with their own privacy practices. Google and GitHub operate internationally and may process information outside the United Kingdom. Their privacy notices describe their processing and transfer safeguards. See Google's Privacy Policy and GitHub's statement above.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the law and circumstances, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or a portable copy of personal information we hold. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact the email above; we may need limited information to identify the record and verify the request. You can also complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office.

Eggy Weggy and updates

The game's device-local saves, optional advertising, age choices, backups and support reports are explained in the Eggy Weggy privacy policy. We will update this website notice if our practices change.
