About this website

Boontang Games is a static informational website about our independent studio and Eggy Weggy. The website currently has no advertising scripts, analytics, accounts, subscription form, or contact form.

Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical access information to maintain the service and protect its infrastructure. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Other websites

Some links lead to external services, including GitHub. These websites have their own privacy policies and practices.

Eggy Weggy privacy policy

The Eggy Weggy Android development preview has its own local saving and optional advertising-related features. Before the game's Google Play release, a separate and accurate policy will be published describing the actual release version, data handling, relevant software development kits, age/consent controls and any purchasing features.

Contact and updates

You can find the studio's current public page at Boontang Games on GitHub. A dedicated support email and complete game privacy information will be added ahead of the public app launch. We will update this website notice if our practices change.
