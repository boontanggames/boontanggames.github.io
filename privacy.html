BOONTANG GAMES · WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy matters.

Clear, straightforward information about this studio website, and what it does — and doesn't — collect.

Please note: This page covers the Boontang Games website only. It is not an Eggy Weggy Android app privacy policy, a Google Play privacy declaration or a substitute for the app's required policy. A separate, accurate game-specific policy must be completed before a public store release.

About this website

boontanggames.com is a static informational website featuring Boontang Games and the Eggy Weggy project. The current site does not contain analytics scripts, advertising SDKs, account sign-in, a newsletter subscription or a contact form.

Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and requests, to operate and secure the service. You can read GitHub's privacy statement.

Links to other services

Some links direct visitors to GitHub. Other websites may have their own policies. We do not control their processing of information.

Eggy Weggy's app privacy

The Android development preview has its own gameplay-saving and optional advertising-related systems. Before publishing on Google Play, Boontang Games will provide a dedicated Eggy Weggy privacy policy describing the actual release build, any included SDKs, relevant data handling, consent and age-related features. This website notice does not describe all those app behaviours.

Contact and updates

The studio's current public profile is Boontang Games on GitHub. A dedicated public support email and full app-policy contact details will be added before a Google Play release. This page will be revised whenever the site's actual data practices change.
